# Fashion-MNIST data card

Source: Zalando Research Fashion-MNIST, loaded through torchvision.datasets.FashionMNIST.

The official training split contains 60,000 grayscale 28×28 images and the official test split contains 10,000 images. Ten clothing categories are represented. The training split is partitioned into 55,000 training and 5,000 validation examples using seed 42. The same splits and normalization are used across the entire compression study. Test accuracy is reported for each predetermined experimental condition.

Small centered grayscale product images have limited resemblance to varied real-world photographs. Benchmark performance does not establish accuracy on photographs with multiple garments, backgrounds or different lighting. The experiment does not collect personal data.
